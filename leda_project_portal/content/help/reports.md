# Reports

Reports turn the data entered in Activities into viewable tables and downloadable files, and are grouped into four categories: Captains Meeting, League Play, Lists and Trails.

Reports are available to Office Admins and Developers. Anyone else who opens the Reports section is sent back to the main Portal page. From the Reports landing page, pick a category card and click **View Available Reports**.

## Captains Meeting Report

![Captains Meeting report selection](/help-images/captains-meeting-reports-menu.png)

Prepare the printed materials for the pre-season captains meeting: folder labels, team reports and the season schedule.

### Features

- **Choose a season.** The **Current Season?** box is checked by default and uses the current season. Uncheck it to pick a different season from the list.
- **Choose a report.** Open the **Report** drop-down (it is enabled once a season is chosen) and pick one of the following.
- **Folder Labels** – one label per team showing the bar name, the division/subdivision/team letters, the team name and the captain's name. The PDF prints the labels in three columns, sorted by bar, then division, subdivision and team letter.
- **Team Report** – one page per team with team info (name, division, subdivision, team letter, and whether the team fee has been paid), bar info (name, address, phone) and a roster table (name, LEDA number, captain, phone, date dues paid, and whether a form is needed). Each page ends with the standard captain notes about fees, adding players and forfeits.
- **Schedules** – the season schedule, shown on screen by division and subdivision in view-only mode (nothing can be edited here). Tick **Detailed View?** in the download box for full matchup details; the default is the compact letter-grid layout.
- **View the data.** Folder Labels and Team Report appear as a table below. Click a column heading to sort, and use the page buttons at the bottom to move through the rows (10 per page).
- **Download.** Once the data has loaded, a **Download PDF** box appears. Click **Download PDF** to save the file. The Schedules report also offers **Download HTML**, a printable web page version.
- **Refresh.** Click the circular-arrow button next to the download buttons to reload the data and rebuild the file if something changed.

### Tips

- If the Download box does not appear, the report has no data for that season.
- Changing the season keeps the selected report but clears the results and reloads them.

## League Play Reports

![League Play report selection](/help-images/league-play-reports-menu.png)

Reports on in-season activity: unpaid fees, missing forms, mentions (awards), Top Darter points, Ton 80 totals and weekly scoresheets.

### Features

- **Choose a season** using **Season** and **Current Season?** (checked by default), then pick a report from the **Report** drop-down. Extra options appear below only for the reports that need them.
- **Bar - Affiliation Fee Not Paid** – bars that have not paid their affiliation fee for the season.
- **Players - No Form** – players with no form on file.
- **Players - Not Paid** – players whose dues are unpaid.
- **Teams - Roster Fee Not Paid** – teams that have not paid their roster fee (shows team, bar and division).
- **Mentions - Best of Division** – Best of Division mentions (player, team, division, mention description and count).
- **Mentions - League Play** – each player's mentions for the season, with the total count and the mention details.
- **Mentions - For Plaques** – players with plaque-qualifying mentions. Enter a **Minimum Mentions** number to limit the list to players with at least that many (the default is 0).
- **Mentions - Ton 80 Weekly League** – each player's cumulative T71 and T80 totals through a week. Choose a **Week** first.
- **Scoresheets - Top Darter Report** – a points leaderboard by player. Enter a **Minimum Points** number to show only players at or above it (the default is 0).
- **Scoresheets - Weekly Scoresheets** – for the chosen week, each team's division, subdivision, previous points, points scored, penalty points and total points, grouped by division and subdivision. Choose a **Week** first.
- **Week list.** The **Week** drop-down shows only weeks that have already been played (dates up to today), as "Week N - date".
- **Minimum box.** After you type a number, wait about a second and the report refreshes. If the box is empty, the report waits for a number.
- **View the data.** Results show in a table. Click a column heading to sort and use the page buttons to move through the rows (10 per page).
- **Download.** When the data has loaded, a **Download PDF** box appears. Click **Download PDF** to save the file. **Top Darter** and **Weekly Scoresheets** also offer **Download HTML**.
- **Refresh.** Use the circular-arrow button to reload the data and rebuild the file.

### Tips

- Weekly Scoresheets and Ton 80 stay blank until you pick a week; Top Darter and Plaques stay blank until the minimum box has a number.
- Download file names include the report, season and (where relevant) week number plus today's date.

## Lists Reports

![Lists report selection](/help-images/lists-reports-menu.png)

Printable lists of captains, election voters, members, places, teams and mailing labels.

### Features

- **Choose a report** from the **Report** drop-down. The options that appear depend on the report.
- **Captains List** – each team captain with phone number, team, division and bar. Choose a season, then leave **All Divisions?** checked or uncheck it and select specific divisions.
- **Election List** – a list of members for the annual election. Choose a **Season** and a **Fiscal Year** (the fiscal year follows the season; the box can be changed once **Current Season?** is unchecked). Use **Good Standing** (on by default) and **Bad Standing** to decide who is included.
- **Members List**, **Places List** and **Team List** – contact and address lists with two ways to filter, chosen with a checkbox:
  - **Filter By Season** (default) – uses the chosen season. Members and Teams can also be limited to selected divisions (**All Divisions?**) and a **Subdivision** range (for example 1 to 99). Places can be filtered by season only.
  - **Filter By Established Date** – pick a **Join Date** and the list is built from that date instead of a season. Members and Places also have **Good Standing** and **Bad Standing** boxes, and Members has **Life Time Member**.
- **Mailing Labels** – a standing list of players and places to mail to. It does not use a season.
  - **Add** – opens a window to choose players and/or places not yet on the list, then **Save**.
  - **Import** – bulk-adds all players, all places, or both (**Import Both**). Choose the type and click **Import**; a progress bar shows while it runs and the number of records imported is shown when done. It can take up to a minute.
  - **Remove one** – hover over a row and click the trash can; you are asked to confirm.
  - **Sorting** – choose **Sort by Name** (default) or **Sort by Zip Code**. The PDF follows this order.
  - The PDF prints three labels across, 30 labels per page.
- **View the data.** Results show in a table; click a heading to sort and use the page buttons (10 rows per page).
- **Download.** When data has loaded, a **Download PDF** box appears. Click **Download PDF**. For Mailing Labels the file is built when you click, which can take a moment. The circular-arrow button reloads the data and rebuilds the file.

### Tips

- Changing any filter clears the results and reloads them.
- With **Filter By Established Date**, nothing shows until a date is chosen.
- If a season has no divisions, the report shows "No divisions available for the selected season."

## Trails Reports

![Trails report selection](/help-images/trails-reports-menu.png)

Reports on the Trails program: points, wins, membership and trip eligibility.

### Features

- **Choose a report** from the **Report** drop-down:
  - **Eligible For Trip** – members with their address and total points, for trip eligibility.
  - **History of Wins** – each player's finishes (1st to 4th) in Singles and Doubles.
  - **Membership List** – the Trails membership history list.
  - **Points List** – each player's previous points, total points, change, last trails date and whether dues are paid.
  - **Save Points Letter** – a letter for each player showing their last trails date and total points. The PDF is formatted as a letter with the LEDA logo and the player's address.
- **View Inactive Players?** – unchecked by default, which hides players whose inactive date has passed. Check it to include them.
- **Trails Points Recorded Since** (Points List only) – a date picker, set to three months ago by default. Players whose last trails date is before this date are left out. Change the date to widen or narrow the list.
- **View the data.** Results show in a table. Click a heading to sort and use the page buttons (10 rows per page).
- **Download.** When data has loaded, a **Download PDF** box appears. Click **Download PDF** to save the file. The Points List also offers **Download HTML**. The circular-arrow button reloads the data and rebuilds the file.

### Tips

- Trails reports do not need a season. They always use the current Trails data.
- Changing **View Inactive Players?** or the date reloads the report.
